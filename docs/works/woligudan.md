# 《我离孤单几公里》 music films

The first work made with agents, before this repository existed: a procedural
skia film for a song by 华晨宇, first set to a newly composed instrumental and
then laid over two recordings of the song. Its source (the score generator,
the mix, the `mv` engine with the walker, the landscape and the survey sheet)
stays in the older `play-forme` workspace on the owner's machine and is not in
this repository; only the renders are archived.

## Versions

| Film | Soundtrack | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 步行谱 (Sheet 73) | an original piece in D major written for the film (no audio from the song) | the owner preferred the original song |
| over the concert instrumental | the 佛山 concert instrumental (arranged by Utopiaoneworllld), film time-warped onto it | rejected: the pictures did not fit that arrangement |
| over the studio recording | the original recording; picture, lyric timing and arc follow it bar by bar | accepted |
